Ermida’ning original ochiq kodini tekshirdim. U yerda 0.6 cm TPW klasslari saqlanadi, 
lekin `TPWpos`dan tanlangan **A, B, C coefficient rastrlari `resample('bilinear')` qilinadi**, keyin LST hisoblanadi. 
Ya’ni original implementationning o‘zi klass chegarasidagi qattiq spatial pog‘onani yumshatishga harakat qiladi. 
Maqolada esa A/B/C koeffitsientlari 0–6 cm oralig‘idagi 10 ta TCWV klass uchun, har 0.6 cm da alohida regressiya bilan kalibrlangani tasdiqlanadi. ([MDPI][1])

Shuning uchun sizning current koddagi:

```text
TCWV
 ↓
TPW bin
 ↓
A/B/C discrete
 ↓
nearest-neighbour
 ↓
LST
```

yo‘li **Ermida original implementationidan farq qiladi**.

Men qiladigan to‘g‘ri yo‘l:

```text
ERA5 TCWV
   ↓
scene vaqtiga temporal interpolation
   ↓
TPW class / TPWpos
   ↓
A/B/C lookup
   ↓
A/B/C .resample('bilinear')
   ↓
Landsat 30 m grid
   ↓
LST = A·Tb/ε + B/ε + C
```

Bu yerda ikkita yaxshi narsa saqlanadi:

1. **Ermida’ning 10 ta discrete TPW klassi buzilmaydi.**
2. Coarse atmosferik grid chegarasida `A/B/C` birdan sakrab qolmaydi.

Ha, mana endi ancha narsani **aniq tiklash mumkin**. Men yuklagan IDC hujjatingizni va Hydrosatning Madera County metodologiyasini solishtirdim. 
Eng muhim topilma: **Hydrosat IDC’ni stock holatda to‘liq ishlatmaydi; 
uni SEBAL bilan birlashtirib, ayniqsa ETni rainfall va applied-water qismlariga ajratish uchun moslashtirgan.**

IDC — **IWFM Demand Calculator**, California DWR’ning root-zone soil-moisture routing va irrigation demand modeli. 
'Siz yuklagan hujjat 2025 Revision 243; Hydrosat esa o‘z hujjatida aynan **IDC 2015.0.77** versiyasiga havola qiladi.
 DWR arxivida bu versiya 2019-yil 20-martda chiqarilgani ko‘rsatilgan. ([Water Resources][1]) 
 Siz yuklagan yangi IDC hujjati ham root-zone routing, runoff, percolation, ET, applied water, ETaw va effective precipitationni alohida bo‘limlarda beradi. 

Eng muhim joy: **Hydrosatdagi IDC vazifasi AW bilan ETAWni ajratib tushunish**.

$$
AW=\text{dalaga berilgan sug‘orish suvi}
$$

ammo

$$
ETAW=\text{ETning aynan sug‘orish suvidan kelgan qismi}
$$

bir xil narsa emas.

Hydrosatning texnik Annexida aniq yozilgan:

$$
\boxed{ET_{aw}=ET-ET_{pr}}
$$

va:

$$
\boxed{ET_{pr}=\alpha ET}
$$

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac{S_{pr}}{S}}
$$

Bu yerda:

* \(S\) = real root-zone storage, unda **rain + irrigation ikkalasi** bor;
* \(S_{pr}\) = faqat **precipitation** bo‘lgan parallel root-zone storage;
* \(\alpha\) = root zonedagi suvning qanchasi rainfall bilan bog‘liq ekanining indikatori;
* \(ET_{pr}\) = rainfall hisobiga bo‘lgan ET;
* \(ET_{aw}\) = irrigation hisobiga bo‘lgan ET. 

Feynmancha, Hydrosat har 10 m pixel uchun **ikki parallel chelak** yuritadi:

```text
REAL BUCKET
P + irrigation
      ↓
      S

RAIN-ONLY BUCKET
faqat P
      ↓
     Spr
```

Keyin:

$$
\alpha=\frac{S_{pr}}{S}
$$

Masalan:

$$
S=100\,mm
$$

$$
S_{pr}=30\,mm
$$

bo‘lsa:

$$
\alpha=0.30
$$

Agar actual SEBAL ET:

$$
ET=6\,mm/day
$$

bo‘lsa:

$$
ET_{pr}=0.30\times6=1.8
$$

$$
ET_{aw}=6-1.8=4.2\,mm/day
$$

Demak **4.2 mm ET sug‘orish suvi hisobidan** deb ajratiladi.

Hydrosat o‘z hujjatida aynan “2 parallel soil moisture buckets for every pixel” deb yozgan. 

### IDC o‘zi bucketni qanday yuritadi?

IDC root-zone state sifatida:

$$
\theta
$$

ya’ni volumetric soil moisture va:

$$
Z
$$

root depthni yuritadi. Asosiy mass-balance ichida precipitation, runoff, applied water, return flow, percolation va ET bor. Hujjat hatto soil moisture’ni manbasiga qarab alohida saqlaydi:

$$
\theta_P
$$

— precipitationdan kelgan moisture,

$$
\theta_{Aw}
$$

— irrigationdan kelgan moisture,

$$
\theta_G
$$

— boshqa manbadan kelgan moisture.

IDC buning sababini aynan **ETning qaysi qismi applied waterdan kelganini kuzatish** deb tushuntiradi. 

Mana bu Hydrosat arxitekturasi bilan juda kuchli mos tushadi.

### Runoffni qanday qiladi?

IDC oddiy fixed CN emas. Modified SCS-CN ishlatadi:

$$
R_P=
\frac{1}{\Delta t}
\frac{(P\Delta t-0.2S)^2}
{P\Delta t+0.8S}
$$

agar yog‘in thresholdni oshsa.

Lekin bu yerdagi retention:

$$
S
$$

soil moisturega bog‘liq.

IDC:

$$
S=S_{max}
$$

quruq holatda, lekin root-zone moisture field capacityning yarmidan oshgach \(S\) kamayadi. Shuning uchun:

```text
soil dry
→ retention katta
→ infiltration ko‘p
→ runoff kam

soil wet
→ retention kichik
→ runoff ko‘p
```

Hujjat aynan moisture field capacityning yarmidan oshgach runoff ortishini aytadi. 

Hydrosatning:

> “surface runoff following a soil moisture deficit and presence of roots; modified SCS”

degani aynan mana shu IDC konsepsiyasiga juda yaqin. 

### Perkolatsiya ham endi aniq

IDC:

$$
P_C=K(\theta)\frac{dh}{dz}
$$

deb boshlaydi.

Vertical gradientni 1 deb oladi va **van Genuchten-Mualem** hydraulic conductivityni ishlatadi. Siz yuklagan PDFda:

$$
\boxed{
P_C=
P_{Crdc}
+
K_s
\left(\frac{\theta}{\theta_T}\right)^{1/2}
\left[
1-
\left(
1-
\left(\frac{\theta}{\theta_T}\right)^{1/m}
\right)^m
\right]^2
}
$$

va:

$$
\boxed{
m=\frac{\lambda}{\lambda+1}
}
$$

Bu yerda:

* \(K_s\) = saturated hydraulic conductivity;
* \(\theta_T\) = total porosity;
* \(\theta\) = current soil moisture;
* \(\lambda\) = pore-size distribution index.

Demak biz oldin “Hydrosat nonlinear Darcian percolation” deganda aynan nimaga o‘xshashini qidirayotgan edik — **IDC hujjatidan formula topildi**. IDC unsaturated vertical percolationni van Genuchten-Mualem orqali qiladi. 

### IDC ETni qanday qiladi?

Stock IDC’da ET satellite’dan kelmaydi.

U:

$$
ET_{pot}
$$

dan boshlaydi va root-zone moisturega qarab stress beradi.

Agar:

$$
\theta>
\frac{\theta_f+\theta_{wp}}{2}
$$

bo‘lsa:

$$
ET=ET_{pot}
$$

Agar moisture FC va WP orasining past yarmiga tushsa:

$$
ET=
2
\frac{\theta-\theta_{wp}}
{\theta_f-\theta_{wp}}
ET_{pot}
$$

va:

$$
\theta<\theta_{wp}
\Rightarrow ET=0
$$

IDC hujjati buni aniq beradi. 

**Lekin Hydrosat aynan shu joyni o‘zgartirgan.**

Hydrosatda:

```text
stock IDC:
θ → stress → ET

Hydrosat:
SEBAL → actual ET
         ↓
    water balance
```

Hydrosat o‘z hujjatida SEBAL surface-energy balance va soil-water balance integratsiyalanganini, **actual ET ikkala balans uchun common denominator** ekanini yozadi. 

Shuning uchun Hydrosatni “IDCni oddiy ishga tushirgan” deb bo‘lmaydi.

Ular ko‘proq:

$$
\boxed{\text{SEBAL actual ET + IDC root-zone physics}}
$$

qilgan.

### Eng qiziq joy — stock IDC’da ham ETaw xuddi source tracking orqali

IDC o‘zi irrigationdan kelgan moisture’ni alohida yuritadi va:

$$
\boxed{
ET_{aw}=\alpha_{Aw}ET
}
$$

qiladi.

\(\alpha_{Aw}\) faqat bugungi irrigation emas; **oldin irrigatsiya orqali tuproqda saqlanib qolgan suv + hozir infiltratsiya qilayotgan irrigation** ulushini ham hisoblaydi. IDC hujjati bu fraction instant contribution va oldingi kunlardan qolgan long-term storage contributionni birgalikda ifodalashini aytadi. 

Precipitation uchun ham parallel:

$$
ET_p=\alpha_P ET
$$

qilinadi. 

Mana Hydrosatning 2 bucket fikri **qayerdan kelgani endi tushunarli**.

## Hydrosat aslida nima qilganini hozir shunday tiklay olamiz

```text
THERMAL SATELLITES
       ↓
    SEBAL 4.0
       ↓
 DAILY ACTUAL ET
       │
       ▼
REAL ROOT-ZONE MODEL
P + irrigation + soil + roots
       ↓
       S
       │
       ├───────────────┐
       │               │
RAIN-ONLY IDC          │
P only                 │
↓                      │
Spr                    │
       │               │
       └──────┬────────┘
              ↓
        α = Spr / S
              ↓
      ETpr = α × ET
              ↓
      ETaw = ET − ETpr
```

Parallel ravishda actual water balance:

$$
\boxed{
AW=ET+R(\theta)+q(\theta)+\Delta S-P
}
$$

ham yuradi. Hydrosat aynan shu formulani beradi. 

Demak **AW va ETAW ikki turli output**:

$$
AW
$$

= dalaga qancha suv berilgan,

$$
ETAW
$$

= o‘sha berilgan suvning qancha qismi ET bo‘lib atmosferaga ketgan.

Shundan irrigation efficiency:

$$
\boxed{
IE=\frac{ETAW}{AW}
}
$$

chiqadi.

## Lekin bitta juda muhim nomuvofiqlik topdim

Hydrosatning batafsil *Measurement Method* hujjati:

$$
\boxed{ET_{pr}=\alpha ET}
$$

$$
\boxed{\alpha=S_{pr}/S}
$$

deydi. 

Ammo alohida Grower Q&A hujjatida:

$$
ETPR=\alpha P
$$

deb yozilgan. ([Madera County Water Resources][2])

Bu ikkalasi **bir xil formula emas**.

Men buni yashirmayman: public Hydrosat hujjatlarining o‘zida shu joyda inconsistency bor.

Texnik Annex esa ikki bucketni, \(S_{pr}/S\)ni va \(ET_{pr}=\alpha ET\)ni batafsil izohlagani uchun **metodologiyani rekonstruksiya qilishda hozir shu batafsil variantni asosiy deb olish mantiqli**, lekin Hydrosatdan final clarification olish yaxshi bo‘ladi.

### Biz uchun eng katta yangilik

Oldin biz:

> “root-zone \(\theta\)ni satellite’dan qanday topamiz?”

deb qidirayotgan edik.

IDC yondashuvida bunga javob boshqacha:

$$
\boxed{\theta\text{ni har kuni satellite’dan bevosita invert qilish shart emas}}
$$

U **state variable** sifatida suv balansida yuritiladi:

```text
θt
+ P
+ AW
- runoff
- ET
- percolation
        ↓
θt+1
```

Satellite SEBAL esa juda kuchli observation beradi:

$$
\boxed{actual\ ET}
$$

Shuning uchun Hydrosat konsepsiyasi **EF→θ empirical formula’dan kuchliroq process-based yo‘l**.

Yana muhim: IDC initial moisture source partitionga juda sezgir bo‘lishi mumkinligi sabab hujjat hatto bir necha yillik **spin-up** tavsiya qiladi.  Hydrosat esa har yili previous water years yordamida soil moisture va root depthni initialize qilishini alohida aytadi. 

Shuning uchun, jigar, **ha — bu IDC hujjati biz qidirayotgan metodologiyaning katta qismini ochib berdi**. Endi Hydrosat usulini deyarli formulama-formula qurishimiz mumkin: `θ routing → moisture-dependent SCS runoff → van Genuchten percolation → S/Spr parallel buckets → ETpr → ETaw → AW`. Faqat Hydrosatning proprietary SEBAL4.0 bilan `actual S`ni aynan qanday constrain/initialize qilishi va ayrim custom IDC o‘zgarishlari to‘liq public emas.

[1]: https://water.ca.gov/Library/Modeling-and-Analysis/Modeling-Platforms/Integrated-Water-Flow-Model-Demand-Calculator?utm_source=chatgpt.com "IDC: Integrated Water Flow Model Demand Calculator"
[2]: https://www.maderacountywater.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Responses-to-Grower-Questions-from-Hydrosat.pdf "2026 Proposal - Questions for RFP Responders (TBC)"


Ha — **bu javob juda muhim**, chunki u Hydrosat arxitekturasini yanada aniq qiladi.

Siz keltirgan gapdan chiqadigan ma’no:

> **Clear thermal day:** ET energy-balance/SEBAL’dan olinadi.
> **Cloudy day:** SEBAL ishlamaydi, ET root-zone water-balance modelidan prognoz qilinadi.

Demak Hydrosat water balance’ni faqat `AW` yoki `ΔS` hisoblash uchun emas, **har kuni uzluksiz ishlaydigan model** sifatida yuritadi.

### Eng mantiqli arxitektura

```text
HAR KUN
  ↓
P + meteorology + crop + soil
  ↓
ROOT-ZONE WATER BALANCE
  ↓
θ(t), S(t), runoff, percolation
  │
  ├── CLEAR DAY
  │      ↓
  │   Satellite thermal
  │      ↓
  │    SEBAL ET
  │      ↓
  │   actual ET bilan state yangilanadi
  │
  └── CLOUDY DAY
         ↓
      θ(t) + ETpot
         ↓
      crop/water-stress model
         ↓
      modeled ET
```

Bu IDC bilan ham juda yaxshi mos tushadi. IDC root-zone moisture’ni vaqt bo‘yicha conservation equation bilan yuritadi va ETni soil moisture holatiga bog‘laydi. 

IDC’ning cloudy-day uchun juda mos bo‘ladigan ET formulasi:

$$
ET =
\begin{cases}
ET_{pot}, &
\theta>\frac{\theta_f+\theta_{wp}}{2}
\\[6pt]
2ET_{pot}
\frac{\theta-\theta_{wp}}
{\theta_f-\theta_{wp}}, &
\theta_{wp}\le\theta\le
\frac{\theta_f+\theta_{wp}}{2}
\\[8pt]
0, & \theta<\theta_{wp}
\end{cases}
$$

Ya’ni:

```text
soil wet enough
→ ET = ETpot

soil drying
→ ET reduced

below wilting point
→ ET = 0
```

IDC aynan shu moisture-stress mantiqni beradi. 

Lekin muhim ehtiyot: **Hydrosat aynan IDC Eq.17 ni verbatim ishlatadi**, deb hozir ayta olmaymiz. Ularning production SEBAL4.0/water-balance integratsiyasi proprietary. Ammo siz bergan “cloudy days → water balance ET” javobi va IDC metodologiyasi birga qaralganda, ular shunga o‘xshash **prognostic root-zone state model** yuritishi juda kuchli ko‘rinadi.

### Bu bizning modelimiz uchun nimani o‘zgartiradi?

Oldin biz:

$$
\theta \leftarrow satellite
$$

deb o‘ylayotgan edik.

Endi yaxshiroq sxema:

$$
\boxed{\theta_t \text{ — model state}}
$$

Har kuni:

$$
\boxed{
S_{t+1}
=
S_t
+
P_t
+
AW_t
-
R_t
-
q_t
-
ET_t
}
$$

Clear day:

$$
\boxed{ET_t=ET_{SEBAL}}
$$

Cloudy day:

$$
\boxed{ET_t=ET_{WB}(\theta_t,ET_{pot},crop)}
$$

bo‘ladi.

Keyin:

$$
\theta_t \rightarrow R(\theta_t)
$$

modified SCS orqali, va:

$$
\theta_t \rightarrow q(\theta_t)
$$

van Genuchten–Mualem orqali hisoblanadi. IDC runoffni moisture-dependent qiladi va percolationni unsaturated hydraulic conductivity orqali hisoblaydi.  

### Eng muhim natija

Hydrosatning “process-based gap filling” degani endi aniqroq:

```text
Landsat/thermal bor
→ SEBAL ET

thermal yo‘q/bulut
→ water-balance ET

yana thermal paydo bo‘ldi
→ SEBAL ET bilan model yana real kuzatuvga bog‘lanadi
```

Bu oddiy linear interpolation emas.

Masalan:

```text
10-iyun  SEBAL ET = 5.2
11-iyun  cloudy → WB ET = 5.0
12-iyun  cloudy → WB ET = 4.7
13-iyun  rain   → θ oshadi
14-iyun  cloudy → WB ET = 5.4
15-iyun  SEBAL ET = 5.1
```

Bu yerda cloudy ETlar **oldingi va keyingi scene orasidagi chiziq emas**; har kunlik precipitation, soil moisture va meteorological demand bilan fizik hisoblanadi.

Shuning uchun bizning yakuniy model ham shunday bo‘lishi kerak:

$$
\boxed{
\text{SEBAL = observation layer}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\text{IDC-like water balance = continuous daily engine}
}
$$

Bu, menimcha, Hydrosat metodologiyasini tushunishdagi eng katta burilish: **water balance yordamchi modul emas — u SEBAL scenelar orasidagi asosiy vaqt motoridir.**
