pySEBAL dagi QO'SHIMCHA ANALITIKALAR (bizda yo'q)
1. ET Decomposition — bug'lanishni bo'laklarga ajratish:

ETpot_24 — potensial ET (Penman-Monteith)
ETref_24 — referens ET (FAO-56 grass)
ET_24_deficit = ETpot - ETact — suv tanqisligi xaritasi
Tact_24 — haqiqiy transpiratsiya (o'simlik)
Eact_24 — haqiqiy evaporatsiya (tuproq)
Tpot_24 — potensial transpiratsiya
T24_deficit = Tpot - Tact — transpiratsiya tanqisligi
beneficial_fraction = Tact/ETact — foydali suv ulushi

2. Crop Coefficients — ekin koeffitsientlari:

kc = ETact / ETref — haqiqiy ekin koeffitsienti
kc_max = ETpot / ETref — maksimal ekin koeffitsienti
Advection_Factor — adveksiya tuzatmasi

3. Soil Moisture — tuproq namligi:

top_soil_moisture — yuza tuproq namligi
root_zone_moisture — ildiz zonasi namligi
SM_stress_trigger — stress chegarasi
moisture_stress_biomass — namlik stress koeffitsienti

4. Biomass & Water Productivity:

FPAR — o'simlik yutgan fotosintez radiatsiyasi ulushi
APAR = FPAR × PAR — yutilgan fotosintez radiatsiyasi
LUE = LUEmax × heat_stress × vapor_stress × moisture_stress
Biomass_prod = APAR × LUE × 0.864 — quruq modda hosildorligi (kg/ha/kun)
Biomass_wp = Biomass / (ET×10) — suv unumdorligi (kg/m³)
Biomass_deficit — potensial vs haqiqiy biomassa farqi
